COOKIE POLICY

Learn which cookies and local storage this website uses and how to control them.

This Cookie Policy explains how the Friends of TUT website ("Website") uses cookies and similar technologies, such as browser local storage, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to remember information about your visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage, perform comparable functions without sending data to a server on every request. Where this policy says "cookies", it includes these similar technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and local storage for the following purposes:

  • Essential (strictly necessary): Remembering your cookie consent choice so we do not ask you on every visit. This is stored in your browser's local storage under the key fot_cookie_consent and is retained for up to 365 days.
  • Analytics (optional): With your consent only, Google Analytics (GA4) and Firebase Analytics help us understand how visitors interact with the Website so we can improve it. These tools collect information such as the pages you visit and the device and browser you use. IP addresses are anonymised where our configuration allows it.
  • Marketing (optional): Reserved for advertising-related measurement. No marketing cookies are set unless you enable them through Cookie Settings.

Essential storage is used because the Website cannot remember your consent choice without it. All other categories require your explicit consent before anything is stored or loaded.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you consent to analytics, the following third parties may set cookies or use similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — operated by Google. See Google's cookie policy and the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.
  • Firebase Analytics — operated by Google. See Firebase privacy information.

These providers may operate servers outside of South Africa. Please review their policies for details on how they process your information.

4. Managing Your Consent

  • Accept All — enables essential, analytics, and marketing cookies.
  • Reject Non-Essential — enables only essential cookies.
  • Customize — choose which optional categories to allow, then save your selection.
  • You can change your mind at any time by using the Cookie Settings button in the footer of every page.
  • You can also clear your browser's storage or block cookies in your browser settings; note that blocking all storage may prevent the Website from remembering your choice, in which case the consent banner will appear again.

5. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
